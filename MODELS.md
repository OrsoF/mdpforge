# Implemented models

This table inventories the 59 model implementations under `models/`. `models/__init__.py` is excluded because it does not define a model. Sources are reported as stated by the implementation; “repository implementation” means that the file names a benchmark family but gives no external bibliographic citation.

| Filename                                | Source                                                                                                                 | Description                                                                                                                                           |
| --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `models/access_control.py`              | Sutton & Barto (2018), _Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction_, 2nd ed., Example 10.2                                | Continuing access-control queue: the state combines free servers and an arriving customer's priority, and the action accepts or rejects the customer. |
| `models/acrobot.py`                     | Sutton (1996), “Generalization in Reinforcement Learning: Successful Examples Using Sparse Coarse Coding”              | Uniformly discretized two-link Acrobot with Euler-integrated deterministic dynamics, three torques, and a terminal target-height condition.           |
| `models/admission.py`                   | User-provided `pyAdmissionControlOF.py`; admission-control / critical-level-policy model of Wieczorek, Busic, and Hyon | Uniformized admission-control CTMDP with customer count, pending class, and hyperexponential service phase; actions accept or reject arrivals.        |
| `models/ambulance.py`                   | Mason & Watts (2012), “Collaborative learning in networks”                                                             | One-dimensional matching model with a beta-distributed next state and reward favoring actions close to the current state.                             |
| `models/ambulance_relocation.py`        | Sinclair, Banerjee & Yu (2019), “Adaptive Discretization for Episodic Reinforcement Learning”                          | Tabular discretization of ambulance relocation on `[0,1]`; actions choose relocation bins and rewards penalize response and relocation distance.      |
| `models/barto.py`                       | Barto, Bradtke & Singh (1995), “Learning to act using real-time dynamic programming”                                   | Stochastic racetrack MDP whose state contains track position and 2-D velocity and whose actions are accelerations.                                    |
| `models/block.py`                       | Meuleau et al. (1998), “Solving very large weakly coupled Markov decision processes”                                   | Random weakly coupled MDP partitioned into sparse-transition blocks with additional cross-block connections.                                          |
| `models/blocks_world.py`                | McCallum (1996), “Whitehead's Blocks World revisited”                                                                  | Factored hand-eye Blocks World in which an agent looks at, picks, and removes colored blocks to uncover and lift a green block.                       |
| `models/car_rental.py`                  | Sutton & Barto (2018), Example 4.2, Jack's Car Rental                                                                  | Two-location inventory MDP with overnight car transfers, Poisson rental requests and returns, rental income, and movement costs.                      |
| `models/cartpole.py`                    | Barto, Sutton & Anderson (1983), classical CartPole; discretized-control benchmarks                                    | Deterministic tabular discretization of cart position/velocity and pole angle/angular velocity with left/right forces.                                |
| `models/chain_walk.py`                  | Lagoudakis & Parr (2003), “Least-Squares Policy Iteration”                                                             | Two-action chain walk with 0.9 intended motion, reflecting boundaries, and rewards at the two endpoints.                                              |
| `models/cliff.py`                       | Sutton & Barto (2018), Example 6.6, Cliff Walking                                                                      | Four-action rectangular cliff gridworld; entering the cliff is penalized and resets the agent to the start.                                           |
| `models/cliff_total.py`                 | Sutton & Barto (2018), Example 6.6, Cliff Walking                                                                      | Total-reward variant of the four-action cliff gridworld.                                                                                              |
| `models/dam.py`                         | Carpentier et al. (2018), “Stochastic decomposition applied to large-scale hydro valleys management”                   | Multi-dam hydro-valley control with reservoir volumes and incoming flows as state and turbine releases as actions.                                    |
| `models/elevator.py`                    | Tartan & Ciflikli (2023), “Sequential Decision Making for Elevator Control”                                            | Compact elevator controller over floor, direction, car destinations, and hall-call masks with move, pickup, and wait actions.                         |
| `models/forest.py`                      | Classical forest-management replacement benchmark                                                                      | Forest-age MDP with actions to wait or cut and stochastic fire risk.                                                                                  |
| `models/frozen.py`                      | Gymnasium/OpenAI Gym `FrozenLake-v1`                                                                                   | FrozenLake gridworld with slippery motion, absorbing holes and goal, and unit reward on reaching the goal.                                            |
| `models/gambler.py`                     | Sutton & Barto, Example 4.3, Gambler's Problem                                                                         | Capital-and-stake MDP with biased coin outcomes, absorbing ruin/goal states, and reward for reaching the goal.                                        |
| `models/garnet.py`                      | Bhatnagar et al. (2009), “Natural actor-critic algorithms”                                                             | Random sparse GARNET MDP with generated transition probabilities and rewards.                                                                         |
| `models/hexagonal_grid_soccer.py`       | Uther & Veloso (1998), Continuous U-Tree; inspired by Littman (1994)                                                   | Single-agent tabular soccer reduction on a hex grid with player, ball, and greedy defender positions.                                                 |
| `models/impatience.py`                  | Hyon & Jean-Marie (2012), “Scheduling services in a queuing system with impatience and setup costs”                    | Queueing-control MDP with stochastic arrivals, impatient customer departures, service decisions, and setup/holding costs.                             |
| `models/inventory.py`                   | Winston & Goldberg (2004), _Operations Research: Applications and Algorithms_, 4th ed.                                 | Stochastic inventory-control MDP with stock/backlog states, replenishment actions, random demand, and operating costs.                                |
| `models/inventory_leadtime.py`          | Repository implementation of an inventory benchmark with one-period delivery lead time                                 | Inventory MDP whose state tracks on-hand stock and an outstanding order; actions place replenishment orders before random demand.                     |
| `models/local_perception_grid_world.py` | McCallum (1996), “Reinforcement Learning with Selective Perception and Hidden State”                                   | Gridworld with concrete cell states and aliased local observations encoded by north/south/east/west wall bits.                                        |
| `models/lqr.py`                         | Repository implementation of a discretized scalar linear-quadratic regulator                                           | Scalar linear system on discretized state/action grids with quadratic state and control costs and noisy transitions.                                  |
| `models/m_maze.py`                      | McCallum (1996), U-Tree / UDM aliased-maze variants                                                                    | M-shaped grid maze whose concrete positions share local wall-pattern observations, producing perceptual aliasing.                                     |
| `models/maintenance.py`                 | Repository implementation of a stochastic maintenance/deterioration benchmark                                          | Machine-condition MDP with deterioration and multiple maintenance intensities trading intervention costs against condition.                           |
| `models/maze_backtrack.py`              | Buck (2015), _Mazes for Programmers_                                                                                   | Four-action grid navigation through a randomly generated recursive-backtracking maze.                                                                 |
| `models/maze_prims.py`                  | Prim (1957), “Shortest connection networks and some generalizations”                                                   | Four-action grid navigation through a randomly generated Prim's-algorithm maze.                                                                       |
| `models/maze_wilson.py`                 | Wilson (1996), “Generating random spanning trees more quickly than the cover time”                                     | Four-action grid navigation through a randomly generated Wilson's-algorithm maze.                                                                     |
| `models/mountain.py`                    | Moore (1990), _Efficient Memory-based Learning for Robot Control_                                                      | Discretized Mountain Car with position/velocity states and three acceleration actions.                                                                |
| `models/ninerooms.py`                   | Inspired by Hengst (2012), “Hierarchical approaches”                                                                   | Stochastic 3-by-3-room gridworld with doors, 0.8 intended motion, and an absorbing exit.                                                              |
| `models/office_world.py`                | Icarte et al. (2018), Office World reward-machine benchmark; CAT+RL usage by Dadvar et al. (2023)                      | Factored gridworld in which the agent collects mail and coffee before reaching the office.                                                            |
| `models/oil.py`                         | Mason & Watts (2012), “Collaborative learning in networks”                                                             | Discrete oil-location model with action-dependent Laplace or quadratic rewards and stochastic discovery-state transitions.                            |
| `models/oil_discovery.py`               | Sinclair, Banerjee & Yu (2019), “Adaptive Discretization for Episodic Reinforcement Learning”                          | Uniform discretization of exploration on `[0,1]`, balancing drilling and movement costs while the latent reservoir drifts.                            |
| `models/option_pricing.py`              | Repository implementation of a finite-horizon American put optimal-stopping MDP                                        | Time/price lattice with exercise and continue actions, binomial price dynamics, and put payoff on exercise or maturity.                               |
| `models/peg_solitaire.py`               | Winston & Goldberg (2004), _Operations Research: Applications and Algorithms_, 4th ed.                                 | Peg-solitaire MDP over board configurations and legal jumps, rewarding the target final configuration.                                                |
| `models/queue_network.py`               | Repository implementation of a controlled queueing-network benchmark                                                   | Finite multi-queue network with bounded queue lengths, stochastic arrivals/services, and server-allocation actions.                                   |
| `models/random_walk.py`                 | Sutton & Barto (2018), random-walk prediction examples                                                                 | Controlled extension of the classical random walk with left/right-biased actions and absorbing endpoint states.                                       |
| `models/replacement.py`                 | Howard (1960), _Dynamic Programming and Markov Processes_, p. 54, “The Replacement Problem”                            | Equipment-age MDP choosing whether to keep or replace an asset under age-dependent returns and deterioration.                                         |
| `models/rooms.py`                       | Hengst (2012), “Hierarchical approaches”                                                                               | Four-room gridworld with internal walls and doorways, four cardinal actions, and doorway/goal rewards.                                                |
| `models/schoolboy.py`                   | Hyon (2020), “Appréhender le hasard,” _Tangente Magazine_                                                              | Finite progress-to-goal MDP with three choices producing different stochastic advances, costs, and setbacks.                                          |
| `models/sutton.py`                      | Sutton & Barto (2018), Exercise 5.12                                                                                   | Racetrack control on a fixed L-shaped track with position/velocity state, acceleration actions, and action failure.                                   |
| `models/swim.py`                        | Strehl & Littman (2008), “An analysis of model-based interval estimation for Markov decision processes”                | RiverSwim chain MDP with a low-reward easy direction and a high-reward hard-to-reach endpoint.                                                        |

| `models/tandem.py`                      | Repository implementation of the cited-in-code tandem-queue experiment (bibliographic citation not given)              | Tandem two-queue system with queue lengths and VM counts in state; nine actions increment/decrement server allocations.                               |

| `models/windgrid.py`                    | Sutton & Barto, Example 6.5, Windy Gridworld                                                                           | Four-action gridworld in which column-dependent upward wind perturbs movement en route to an absorbing goal.                                          |
